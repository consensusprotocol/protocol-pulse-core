Today's Brief

Dormant supply begins to stir.

Analysis of UTXOs aged five years or older shows a twelve percent week-over-week increase in on-chain movement — the sharpest uptick since March of last year. This cohort, historically the most steadfast of holders, is beginning to distribute at the exact moment spot ETF inflows turned positive again.

That is not, by itself, a bearish signal. Long-term holders distribute on strength. The question this brief answers is whether the distribution is measured (typical of a healthy bull continuation) or accelerating (typical of a local top).

Our Convergence Engine weights today's reading as a medium-term bearish divergence. But the on-chain signal is strong, macro is neutral, and sentiment is constructive. Three out of three, except for this one thread.
