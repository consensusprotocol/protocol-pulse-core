Cypherpunk Stack

Freedom Technology

Bitcoin proved that trustless, permissionless systems can replace centralized gatekeepers. That was round one. Now we apply the same mathematics to communication, identity, data, and money. Every tool below is a step toward a life that requires no one's permission.

"We must defend our own privacy if we expect to have any. We must come together and create systems which allow anonymous transactions to take place."

— A Cypherpunk's Manifesto, Eric Hughes, 1993
📱
Signal
The gold standard of private messaging
Comms

Your IP, metadata, and contact graph are not stored. End-to-end encrypted by default. No ads, no data mining — it's a nonprofit with no business model that requires selling you.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Download from signal.org — not the App Store if you can help it
  2. 2Enable disappearing messages by default in Settings → Privacy
  3. 3Verify contacts via Safety Numbers for high-stakes conversations
  4. 4Use Note to Self as an encrypted scratchpad

Nostr
Censorship-resistant social. Owned by no one.
Comms

No servers to shut down. No accounts to ban. Your identity is a cryptographic keypair — you bring it to any client. Jack Dorsey uses it. That should tell you something.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Generate keys at nostr.com or use Primal / Damus app
  2. 2Save your nsec (private key) somewhere safe — lose it and you lose your identity
  3. 3Connect via Primal (iOS/Android) or Snort.social (web)
  4. 4Zap sats to content you value — it's built in

Tor Browser
Your IP is not your identity
Privacy

Routes your traffic through three volunteer-run relays. No single relay knows both who you are and what you're accessing. Built by the US Navy, trusted by journalists and dissidents worldwide.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Download from torproject.org — verify the signature
  2. 2Use bridges if Tor is blocked in your region
  3. 3Don't login to personal accounts — that defeats the purpose
  4. 4For maximum anonymity use Tails OS instead

Tails OS
A computer with amnesia — by design
Privacy

Boots from a USB drive. Routes everything through Tor. Leaves zero trace on the hardware when shut down. Used by Snowden. The nuclear option for operational security.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Download ISO from tails.net and verify the signature
  2. 2Flash to a USB drive (8GB+) using Balena Etcher
  3. 3Boot from USB — F12 / Del on startup for boot menu
  4. 4Enable Persistent Storage to save files across sessions

GPG / GNU Privacy Guard
Encrypt anything. Sign everything.
Privacy

The original cypherpunk tool. Public-key cryptography for email, files, and identity verification. Math protects your messages — not policy, not promises, not terms of service.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Install: brew install gnupg or sudo apt install gnupg
  2. 2Generate keypair: gpg --full-gen-key
  3. 3Share your public key — keep your private key offline

Pi-hole
Block every tracker on your entire network
Privacy

Network-level DNS sinkhole that blocks ads and trackers before they reach any device in your home. Your Smart TV, your kids' tablets, your phone — all protected at the router level.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Install on Raspberry Pi or any Linux box
  2. 2Set as your router's primary DNS server
  3. 3Add blocklists at firebog.net for maximum coverage

IPFS
Content-addressed storage. Can't be taken down.
AI & Tech

Files on IPFS are addressed by what they contain, not where they're stored. There is no server to seize, no domain to suspend. The same principle as Bitcoin's blockchain — just for files.

Difficulty
Privacy
Visit
Get Started
  1. 1Install IPFS Desktop from ipfs.tech
  2. 2Pin files to your local node
  3. 3Share via ipfs.io/ipfs/[hash] gateways
