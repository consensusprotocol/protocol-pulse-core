// Jevons Paradox

Lower Cost = Exploding Demand

When you lower the cost of something that is supply-constrained, demand doesn't stay the same. It explodes. William Stanley Jevons noticed this with coal in 1865. The same dynamic is playing out with AI today.

Software engineering used to cost $200K/year per engineer. At $20/month for Claude, the demand for software doesn't fall — it multiplies by 100×. Fortune 500 IT spend at 5% of revenue should be 50%.
