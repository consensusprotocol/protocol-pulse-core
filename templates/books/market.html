How to read the map

Cover area = normalized rank strength · Horizontal = publication age (log scale) · Vertical = momentum

  • Cover area grows with rank strength: the best illustrative rank on the map is the largest cover. Covers keep their own shape.
  • Left to right: publication age of the tracked edition on a log scale, older to the left. Recent years get more room, so equal distances are not equal time. Year marks sit on the same scale. Positions use the publication year; the details give the exact date where the source records it.
  • Up: rank improved over 7 days. Down: rank cooled.
  • A dot marks each book's true position, drawn above the covers. A cover moved aside to avoid an overlap (at most {{ (m.map.cap_fraction * 100)|round|int }}% of each axis) is joined to its dot by a thin line.
  • Only print editions ranked in Amazon US Books are plotted. Different lists are never mixed.
