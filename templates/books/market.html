How to read the map

Cover area = normalized rank strength · Horizontal = publication recency · Vertical = momentum

  • Cover area grows with rank strength: the best illustrative rank on the map is the largest cover. Covers keep their own shape.
  • Left to right: publication year of the tracked edition, established to newer. Recent years get more room (log scale of age).
  • Up: rank improved over 7 days. Down: rank cooled.
  • A thin line marks a cover moved aside to avoid an overlap; the dot is its true position.
  • Only print editions ranked in Amazon US Books are plotted. Different lists are never mixed.
