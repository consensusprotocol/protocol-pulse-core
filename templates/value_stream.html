What is Commander?
Commander is Protocol Pulse's premium intelligence tier. You get the Pulse Terminal (Bloomberg-style real-time dashboard), the AI Signal Engine with live alerts, Priority Oracle access, on-chain analytics, and Lightning intelligence — everything a serious Bitcoiner needs to stay ahead.
How is it different from the free tier?
Free Agent gives you daily briefs, basic charts, and standard Oracle access. Commander unlocks the full real-time terminal, the signal engine with push alerts, priority Oracle responses, and advanced on-chain and Lightning data.
Can I pay with Bitcoin?
Currently we accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Bitcoin payment is available for the Sovereign tier on request. Commander Bitcoin payment is coming soon.
What is the Oracle?
The Oracle is Protocol Pulse's AI analyst. It processes real-time market data, on-chain signals, sentiment feeds, and news to answer your Bitcoin questions with sourced, data-backed analysis. Commander users get priority access with faster, deeper responses.
