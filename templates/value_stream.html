Frequently Asked Questions

What is the AI Signal Score?
Our proprietary scoring engine analyzes sentiment, on-chain flows, mempool data, macro indicators, and social signals in real-time to produce a single 0-100 directional score updated every 2 minutes.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Commander is billed monthly with no contracts. Cancel from your account page and you keep access through the end of your billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Bitcoin payments for Sovereign tier available on request.
How is Sovereign different from Commander?
Sovereign includes private Oracle AI briefings, unlimited API access, custom alert configurations, institutional-grade macro intelligence, a dedicated Telegram channel, and scheduled 1-on-1 sessions with our analysts.
Is there a free trial for Commander?
The Free Agent tier gives you permanent access to core intelligence. Upgrade to Commander when you need the full signal suite — no trial needed because you can evaluate the platform risk-free.
What kind of API access is included?
Commander includes REST API access with 1,000 requests per day covering price, mempool, sentiment, signal scores, and more. Sovereign unlocks unlimited requests and webhook integrations.
