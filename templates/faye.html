Three rules. Memorize them. They will save your money.

  1. Anyone asking for your seed phrase, PIN, or wallet access is a scammer. No exceptions. Ledger will never ask. Trezor will never ask. Your exchange will never ask. Real support never needs your keys.
  2. Do not trust messages that come to you. Not email. Not phone calls. Not SMS. Not DMs. Not chat support. If they reached out first, assume it's fake until you verify it independently — by typing the company's URL yourself or calling the number on the back of your card.
  3. "Urgent security problem with your account" is a red flag, every time. Real companies don't pressure you. They don't threaten loss of access. They don't have 24-hour countdowns. If it feels rushed, walk away.
