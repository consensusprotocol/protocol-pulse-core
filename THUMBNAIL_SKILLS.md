## Core Universal Rules (Always Apply)

### 1. The 0.3 Second Rule
Eyes decide in 300ms whether to click or scroll. Design for the snap judgment, not the considered view. Ask: what is the ONE thing this thumbnail communicates instantly?

### 2. Text Overlay Best Practices
- 3-4 words MAX on thumbnail text (3x CTR boost vs. 7+ words)
- Font: Bold, sans-serif (Bebas Neue, Impact, or similar)
- Minimum size: readable at 120×90px (mobile feed size — THE standard)
- Contrast ratio: minimum 4.5:1 (accessibility = visibility)
- Text placement: bottom-left or bottom-right, never center (face needs center)
- Avoid text at edges (platform UI can cut it)

### 3. Color Psychology for CTR
- **Red** (#dc2626, #CC0000): Urgency, danger, important — highest CTR trigger
- **Yellow** (#f5c518, #FCD34D): Warning, exciting, money — pairs perfectly with red/black
- **Orange** (#F7931A): Bitcoin orange — use for BTC-specific content
- **White**: Contrast on dark backgrounds — always readable
- **Black backgrounds**: Premium, authority, mystique — Protocol Pulse standard
- AVOID: Pastels, light blues, greens for news/finance (low urgency perception)
- Best combo: Red text + Yellow accent + White on Black = highest CTR for finance

### 4. Face & Emotion Impact
- Human faces increase CTR by 25-40% (Nielsen eye-tracking research)
- Emotions that drive clicks: Shock/Surprise > Fear > Anger > Excitement > Curiosity
- Eyes looking AT camera > eyes looking away (direct engagement)
- Exaggerated expressions outperform neutral by 2-3×
- For financial/Bitcoin content: Shock/disbelief + money visual = optimal combo

### 5. Heat Map Priorities (Where Eyes Go First)
1. Face (top-left or center)
2. Large text overlay
3. Logo/branding (bottom corner)
Design in this order: Face → Text → Logo

### 6. The Numbers Rule
- Numbers in thumbnails increase CTR by 36% (BuzzSumo research)
- Specific numbers beat round ones: "$47,382" beats "$50,000"
- Percentages + direction: "+340%" or "−17%" are strong triggers
- BTC price at key levels (ATH, crash, round numbers) always works

## Title Formula Patterns

### High-CTR Title Templates
1. **The Curiosity Gap**: "Why Bitcoin Is About to [THING] (And Nobody's Talking About It)"
2. **The Number List**: "7 Signs Bitcoin Hits $[X] Before [DATE]"
3. **The Contradiction**: "Everyone Says [X]. Here's Why They're Wrong."
4. **The Insider**: "What The Fed Isn't Telling You About [TOPIC]"
5. **The Urgency**: "This Changes Everything For Bitcoin Holders"
6. **The Warning**: "Bitcoin Warning: [SPECIFIC THREAT] Is Real"
7. **The Analysis**: "I Analyzed [X] [THINGS] — Here's What I Found"

### Title Rules
- 50-60 characters max (YouTube cuts at ~70, but 55 is optimal for search + click)
- Front-load the hook: most important word in positions 1-3
- "How/Why/What" outperform declarative titles by 15-25%
- Curiosity gap: imply value without revealing the answer
- Numbers increase CTR by 36% (use them whenever legitimate)
- Avoid clickbait that doesn't deliver — retention drop kills distribution

## Bitcoin-Specific Thumbnail Rules

### Bitcoin Price Visual
1. BTC price in thumbnail when it's dramatic (ATH, crash, round number)
2. Orange (#F7931A) for Bitcoin logo/accent — always use Bitcoin's actual color
3. Show a chart with a dramatic line (up or down — both work)
4. Combine: BTC logo + dramatic price + shocked face = peak CTR
5. "₿" symbol or Bitcoin logo in corner builds brand recognition over time

### Bitcoin Content Categories
- **Bull run content**: Orange/gold, upward chart, excited face, "ATH" text
- **Bear market content**: Red, downward chart, concerned face, % drop
- **Policy/regulation**: Blue-red contrast, government building, urgency text
- **Technology**: Dark bg, code/circuit aesthetic, green terminal text
- **Mining**: Industrial imagery, black/orange, power/hash rate numbers
- **Lightning**: Yellow/orange lightning bolt, speed emphasis, low sat fees

### Protocol Pulse Brand Standards
- Black background (#000000) always — premium, authoritative
- Red accent (#dc2626 / #CC0000) — urgency, our brand color
- JetBrains Mono for data overlays — our signature mono font
- Logo: bottom-right corner, small — brand without taking over
- "PROTOCOL PULSE" text: white, mono font, subtle — not the hero

## A/B Testing Insights (From Major Creators)

### MrBeast Team Techniques
- Tests 20-40 thumbnail variations before publishing
- Primary variable: face vs. no face
- Secondary variable: text size and position
- Tertiary: background color/environment
- Decision: whichever gets highest CTR in first 30 minutes gets the slot

### Finance/News Creator Best Practices (Graham Stephan, Andrei Jikh et al.)
- Number in thumbnail almost always beats no number
- Self in thumbnail almost always beats no self
- Contrast between "before" and "after" visual = high performer
- Fake shock expression outperforms genuine emotion by 2× (counterintuitive)

### Bitcoin-Specific Creator Insights (Bitcoin Magazines, Pomp, Anthony Pompliano)
- BTC price + chart >> talking head alone
- "Breaking news" style overlay triggers urgency
- Institutional logos (BlackRock, Fidelity) as props = authority signal
- Political figures (presidents, Fed chairs) as props = controversy = clicks

## Mobile-First Optimization (PRIORITY)

- 70%+ of YouTube views come from mobile devices
- Design at 1280×720 but preview at 120×90 before publishing
- Thumbnail must communicate in 2 elements at mobile size: face + ONE word
- No fine print. No complex charts. No small logos.
- Bold. Simple. One message. Maximum contrast.
