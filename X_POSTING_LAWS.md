## THE PRINCIPLE

Every post serves one of three purposes:
1. **Signal** — Deliver genuinely useful Bitcoin intelligence
2. **Identity** — Reinforce Protocol Pulse as the sovereign intelligence brand
3. **Growth** — Drive followers, subscribers, and engagement

If a post doesn't serve at least one of these, don't post it.

## SECTION 1: PLATFORM ALGORITHM RULES (verified behavior, not speculation)

### What X rewards:
- Native content (text, images, video uploaded directly to X)
- Replies and conversations (high reply ratio = signal to algorithm)
- Time spent reading (longer dwell time on your post = boost)
- Bookmarks (strongest engagement signal after replies)
- Posts that generate quote tweets (viral amplification)
- Consistent posting cadence (daily activity > sporadic bursts)
- Threads (multi-tweet threads get boosted if first tweet hooks well)

### What X suppresses:
- External links in the main post body (YouTube, Substack, websites)
  **CONFIRMED: X actively throttles impressions for posts containing outbound URLs.**
  Our data shows URL tweets at 3x engagement, but those are MEDIA ATTACHMENTS
  (images/video auto-wrapped in t.co), NOT external links. Do not confuse these.
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree", "RT to spread the word")
- Duplicate content across accounts
- Posts that get hidden/reported frequently
- New accounts with sudden high-volume posting

### The External Link Workaround:
When you MUST link externally (to a YouTube video, article, etc.):
- NEVER put the link in the main tweet
- Post the content as a native tweet (text + image/video)
- Add the link as the FIRST REPLY to your own tweet
- Pin the reply or add "Link in replies" at the end of the main tweet
- This preserves algorithm reach on the main tweet while still delivering the link

## SECTION 3: PROTOCOL PULSE POSTING RULES

### The Daily Cadence:
- **6-8 AM ET**: Morning intelligence tweet (data-first, what happened overnight)
- **11 AM-1 PM ET**: Mid-day insight (analysis, not just news)
- **4-6 PM ET**: Episode promotion (if daily video published) — link in reply, not main tweet
- **8-10 PM ET**: Community engagement (reply to partner channels, quote tweet with analysis)
- **Total: 4-6 posts per day.** Quality over quantity. Never exceed 8.

### Content Mix (per week):
- 40% — Original intelligence (data, on-chain metrics, market analysis)
- 25% — Commentary on breaking Bitcoin news (be FIRST with a smart take)
- 15% — Community engagement (quote tweets, replies to Bitcoiners)
- 10% — Episode/article promotion (always link in reply)
- 10% — Brand personality (behind-the-scenes, cypherpunk philosophy)

### Formatting Rules:
- Max 120 characters for single tweets. Under 280 always.
- Lead with the number: "1,056 EH/s — Bitcoin just hit all-time high hashrate."
- Use line breaks for emphasis. Short paragraphs. One idea per tweet.
- No hashtags in the main body (they look spammy). Max 1 hashtag, at the end, if any.
- No em dashes (—) unless it's a Lyn Alden-style analytical connector.
  **DATA CHECK:** Em dash usage did NOT correlate with higher engagement in our study.
  Use sparingly and naturally, not as a style crutch.
- No emojis except 🟢/🔴 for price direction and ⚡ for Lightning Network.
- Never start with "Just" or "So" or "Honestly" or "Wow."
- Never end with "Thoughts?" (engagement bait, algorithm penalizes)

### Media Rules:
- Image tweets get 2-3x the impressions of text-only (X rewards native media)
- Use chart screenshots (TradingView, Glassnode) with a key insight highlighted
- Use the Protocol Pulse branded template for data graphics
- Video clips from the daily episode: upload as native video, never as YouTube link
- Max 45 seconds for native video (X sweet spot for completion rate)

### Thread Rules:
- Threads for deep analysis only. Max 5 tweets.
- First tweet must stand alone as a complete insight (don't start with "Thread:")
- Each subsequent tweet adds value (no filler, no "let me explain")
- Last tweet: CTA to subscribe/follow + link in reply
- Threads get 2x the top-performer rate. Use 2-3x per week.

### Reply Strategy:
- Reply to every partner channel's Bitcoin tweets within 1 hour
- Add genuine analysis, not "great post!" sycophancy
- Quote tweet partner content with a Protocol Pulse angle
- Goal: become the account that Saylor's followers see in every reply section

## SECTION 4: BANNED PRACTICES

- NEVER put external links in main tweet body
- NEVER use more than 1 hashtag per tweet
- NEVER post engagement bait ("Like and RT if...")
- NEVER post without data or a specific insight (no vague "Bitcoin is the future")
- NEVER copy another account's take without adding original analysis
- NEVER post more than 8 times in a day (algorithm flags as spam)
- NEVER use threads longer than 5 tweets (completion rate drops off a cliff)
- NEVER post the same content to X and Nostr verbatim (platform-specific formatting)
- NEVER auto-post without human review (AI-generated tweets must pass PBX approval until
  the voice is calibrated and tested over 20+ manual reviews)

## SECTION 5: NOSTR ADAPTATION

Nostr has different rules than X:
- External links are NOT penalized (decentralized, no algorithm suppression)
- Longer-form content works well (NIP-23 long-form articles)
- Community is smaller but more engaged and Bitcoin-native
- Zaps (Lightning tips) are the engagement metric, not likes
- Cross-post the INTELLIGENCE, not the exact tweet. Rewrite for the audience.
- Nostr posts can be more technical, more cypherpunk, more philosophical
- Include npub and relay information in profile

### Nostr Posting Cadence:
- Mirror the daily intelligence tweet (rewritten for Nostr audience)
- Post episode link with full description (links welcome on Nostr)
- Engage in Nostr-specific discussions (relay management, NIP proposals, privacy)
- 2-3 posts per day on Nostr. Quality over quantity.

- Monitor reach/impression changes on Protocol Pulse's own posts weekly

  investigate algorithm shift and update Section 1

After 50 posts, begin structured testing:

- Week 5-6: Thread vs single tweet for same content → measure completion
- Log results in data/x_posting_performance.json
- Update laws with statistically significant findings (minimum 20 samples per variant)

## SECTION 7: AUTOMATION READINESS

Posts are NOT auto-published until:
1. PBX has manually reviewed and posted 50+ tweets following these rules
2. Engagement rate is tracking at or above the median of partner accounts
3. The tweet voice is calibrated (confirmed by PBX, not assumed by AI)
4. A human review step exists in the automation pipeline

Automation phases:
- **Phase 1 (now)**: AI drafts tweets → PBX reviews and posts manually
- **Phase 2 (after 50 posts)**: AI drafts + schedules → PBX approves batch daily
- **Phase 3 (after 200 posts + consistent engagement)**: AI auto-posts routine content
  (morning data briefs, episode promos) → PBX reviews weekly
- **Phase 4 (after 500 posts)**: Full autonomy with quality gate (engagement < threshold
  triggers hold for human review)
