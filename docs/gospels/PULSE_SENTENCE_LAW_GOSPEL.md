# PULSE CHECK SENTENCE LAW GOSPEL (PBX review of run pinned_20261110_150345, 2026-11-11)

## WHAT THIS FEATURE IS
The automated Protocol Pulse "Pulse Check" medley (pipeline/run_fresh.py) cuts hooks and clips from
partner YouTube videos, renders 16:9 and 9:16, and runs QA gates. PBX watched the 2026-11-10 cut and
found editorial errors every gate had passed: the opening word sounded clipped (D1), clips started or
ended mid-sentence (D2/D3), credit avatars were warped (D4), the vertical was letterboxed (D5) and
channel changes were abrupt (D6). This work makes the gates catch what a human caught, proves it on
yesterday's files (tests/fixtures/pulse_20261110, pipeline/test_pulse_20261110_mustfail.py), fixes the
causes, then requires three clean fresh videos in a row with no code edits between them.

## THE LAWS
- SENTENCE LAW: a clip's first word is the first word of a sentence and its last word the last word of
  a sentence. Boundary = transcript punctuation at a place a listener hears as the break between the two
  words (DEC-10, 2026-11-11: no minimum pause; the 01:50 order's 80 ms is recalibrated to the approved v2f
  reference, whose edges measure 0-10 ms; pipeline/sentence_law.py states every constant with its
  measurement). An unpunctuated transcript stretch has NO boundary
  (fail closed). Punctuation is added at transcription time only from a second, prompted ASR decode of
  the same audio, onto aligned verbatim words; the words never change (pipeline/punctuation.py). No
  language model decides a boundary (the per-gap adjudicator called 19/19 gaps sentence ends; removed).
- SELECTION LOCK: the words a clip must contain are fixed at selection time (selection_lock in edl.json,
  sha256-sealed). No later stage may recompute expected first/last words from a moved cut.
- SNAP LIMIT: a cut may move only inside the heard gap between the chosen sentence and its neighbour
  (the previous word's offset .. the next word's onset, at speech - 6 dB); it may never add or drop a word.
  A short gap is cut at the envelope minimum between the two words, never rejected for its length (DEC-10 d);
  its fade sits inside the gap (L-b: 2 video frames = 83 ms when it fits, down to 10 ms).
- NEIGHBOUR (DEC-10 c-ii): NO audible part of the neighbouring word: every frame the cut keeps on the
  neighbour's side of that minimum stays below the valley threshold (local speech - 12 dB) after the fade.
  A window is rejected for its edges only if this, the 85 % edge-word rule or sentence-completeness fails.
- ONSET/TAIL: edge words keep >= 85 % of their duration in the render (a floor for timestamp noise, never
  permission to trim: L-b below). The START OF PLAYBACK keeps the D1
  rule unchanged: a source that starts speaking at its file head, and the hook, need >= 80 ms of genuine
  sub-speech audio before the first onset or a declared room-tone lead-in pad; codec zero/padding is not
  room tone. Inside the video there is no lead / tail minimum (DEC-10: v2f's approved edges: 0 ms).
- CREDITS: round avatars of the channels actually used; the avatar is the channel's real square avatar,
  never a video thumbnail, never a generated placeholder; G12 / G15 measure the brand-red rings the renderer
  draws and G15 checks each disc is the credited channel's registered avatar (DEC-11).
- L-a SIGN-OFF NAMES ALL (PBX, 2026-11-11): the sign-off voiceover names every channel on the credits card, and the spoken set, the card set and the clips used are identical.
  Check: run_fresh.stage_voice HOLDs before render unless the script names exactly credit_rings.credit_channels(edl) (hook first, then clips); G20 SIGNOFF_NAMES_ALL transcribes the rendered sign-off and requires spoken == credited (registry names + aliases); VO stems are keyed by hash(text, voice, model). Must-fail: the 2026-11-11 preview (DataDash missing). Must-pass: v2f.
- L-b EDGE WORDS WHOLE (PBX, 2026-11-11): a fade never overlaps an edge word: the first and last word of every clip are heard whole, at full level, with their natural decay.
  Check: sentence_law.edge_fades places each machine-cut fade (2 frames, shortened down to 10 ms) inside the gap next to the cut, measured from the cut point's level; G17c (sentence_law.gate_g17_edges) requires every frame of both edge words within 1 dB of the unfaded source.   Check: run_fresh.stage_gates: a dissenting G3b reading (DEC-12f) or a G3c head / tail failure on an edge stands (HOLD / FAIL) unless G17c and G16's neighbour_clear both pass for that edge.

- LAW 20 / M2: gate thresholds are constants; a failed gate is a hard stop; never change what a check
