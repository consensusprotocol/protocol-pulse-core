What is Nostr?

Nostr (Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays) is a decentralized communication protocol built for censorship resistance. Unlike X (Twitter) or Facebook, no single company can ban you, shadow-ban your account, or decide what you're allowed to say — your identity is a cryptographic keypair you control.

Bitcoin's cypherpunk community has adopted Nostr as its primary uncensorable layer. Developers, analysts, and OGs post raw signal here that surfaces 15–30 minutes before mainstream crypto media catches on. Engagement is measured in zaps — actual Bitcoin payments via Lightning that represent real conviction, not empty likes.

Protocol Pulse monitors Nostr continuously, scoring every post by engagement quality (zaps 10× > reposts 3× > replies 2× > reactions 1×) and Bitcoin relevance, then surfaces the top signal for you here — updated every 5 minutes.
