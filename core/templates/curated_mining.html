Tax Strategy

The Section 179 Advantage

DEDUCT UP TO $1,160,000 IN YEAR 1

Under IRC Section 179, qualifying Bitcoin mining equipment may be fully expensed in the year of purchase — dramatically reducing your effective cost basis.

  • Full purchase price deductible in year one (up to $1.16M limit, 2024 figure)
  • LLC structure ensures deductions pass through to your personal return
  • Typical clients reduce effective equipment cost by 25–37% through year-one deductions
  • Bonus depreciation may provide additional deductions — consult your CPA
